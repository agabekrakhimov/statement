      Weekly routine (20 minutes)
      1. Statement day: upload every truck's PDF above. No truck should show “Missing”.
      2. Red flags: anything critical, call or email the carrier the same day and ask for the correction in writing.
      3. Rate vs cost: a truck below its cost per mile means a talk with dispatch about the loads they booked.
      4. Fuel: low MPG means a talk with the driver about idling, speed and tires.
      5. One-time charges: every one needs a receipt that matches your truck number.
      6. Tick off finished actions and press “Get coaching”.
      7. Monthly: compare carriers on the Compare tab and top up the repair fund on the Trucks tab.
