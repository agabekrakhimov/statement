        Action plan

        Built automatically from your statements. Tick items off as you do them; ticks are saved. New items appear when new statements reveal something.

          Your weekly routine (20 minutes)

          1. Day statements arrive: upload every truck's PDF on the This week tab. Make sure no truck shows “Missing”.
          2. Read the red flags. Anything “critical” = call or email the carrier the same day. Ask for corrections in writing.
          3. Check each truck's RPM vs CPM. Below cost = talk to dispatch about the loads they booked.
          4. Check MPG and fuel $/gal. Low MPG = talk to the driver (idling, speed, tires).
          5. Look at one-time charges. Every one needs a receipt that matches your truck number.
          6. Tick off finished actions here and press “Get coaching” for next week's priorities.
          7. Monthly: compare carriers on the Dashboard (fee %, weekly fees, fuel discount) and set aside money for maintenance, plates and IFTA.

          🚩 Red flags

          Checked automatically on every statement: totals that don't add up, driver pay that doesn't match the loads, one-time and unexplained charges, losing weeks, MPG under 6.5, deadhead over 15%, loads below cost per mile, maintenance over $0.10/mi, missing statements.
